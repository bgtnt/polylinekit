Why measure changed area, rather than total area?

Same total area. A different shape. In this fixed analytic control, a half-unit notch and a half-unit bump cancel: both regions have area 32. The changed area is still 1, and its Jaccard distance is 1 / 32.5 = 3.0769%.

This explanatory pair is specified directly; it is not a claim about a candidate generated by the simplifier. Choose “Added and removed area” to try the actual simplification flow.

Clipper2 generates the candidates. PolylineKit measures each against the unchanged original using NonZero fill. The chosen result has the fewest vertices among the measured candidates within your budget, with deterministic ties. It is not a globally optimal contour.

A small changed area does not bound the largest boundary displacement or guarantee preserved topology. Thin details can disappear. This demo accepts one closed contour, including self-intersections, with at most 1,024 points.

Areas use squared coordinate units. The Natural Earth presets use a normalized local map plane; these numbers are not square kilometres. Resizing the preview changes neither coordinates nor measurements.
